Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how lstzcx (the "Widget", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use it through Discord.

1. Information We Collect

From Discord (when you authorize or use the Widget): - Your Discord user ID, username, display name, and avatar - Server (guild) IDs and names where the Widget is installed - Channel IDs and role information, if needed for the Widget to function - Any other data covered by the permissions/scopes you grant (e.g., identify, guilds)

Data you provide: - Settings, inputs, or content you submit to the Widget: Just to show off cool stuff

Automatically collected: - Basic technical and log data (e.g., timestamps, errors, command usage) used to keep the Widget running and to fix problems

We do not collect your Discord password, payment information, or private messages.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information to: - Operate and provide the Widget's features - Identify you and apply your settings - Maintain, secure, and improve the Widget - Prevent abuse and enforce our Terms of Service - Respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

3. How We Share Information

We only share information: - With service providers that host or operate the Widget (e.g., [lastmine]), under appropriate confidentiality - When required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and security - With your consent

The Widget runs on Discord, so Discord's own handling of your data is governed by the Discord Privacy Policy.

4. Data Retention

We keep your data only as long as needed to provide the Widget. When you remove the Widget from a server or request deletion, we delete the related data within 30 days, except where we must keep it for legal or security reasons.

5. Your Rights and Choices

You can: - Revoke the Widget's access at any time in Discord (User Settings → Authorized Apps) - Ask us what data we hold about you - Request correction or deletion of your data

To make a request, contact lastmine on discord. Depending on where you live (e.g., EU/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA), you may have additional rights, and we will honor them as required by law.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Children's Privacy

The Widget is not intended for anyone under 13 (or the minimum age Discord requires in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International Transfers

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, which may have different data protection laws. By using the Widget, you consent to this where permitted by law.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version. Continued use of the Widget means you accept the changes.
